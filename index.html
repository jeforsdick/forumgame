Ms. Rivera’s Classroom

Teacher

Open to support, motivated, overwhelmed in the moment

Student Context

Escalation most likely during transitions

Current Challenge

Plan exists, but implementation is inconsistent

Ms. Rivera is a general education teacher supporting a student with an individualized, function-based behavior support plan. The student has the most difficulty during transitions, especially when independent work ends and the class shifts to a new activity. Ms. Rivera wants to support the student well and is open to coaching, but she says she feels overwhelmed in the moment and has trouble remembering how to use all parts of the plan consistently when the classroom gets busy.
